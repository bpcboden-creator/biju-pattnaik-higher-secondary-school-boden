ABOUT THE SCHOOL

A place to learn, explore and grow.

Biju Pattnaik Higher Secondary School, Boden, District Nuapada, provides higher secondary education with opportunities for students across Arts, Science and Commerce.

Our focus is on strong academic foundations, practical learning, responsible citizenship and an encouraging school environment.
